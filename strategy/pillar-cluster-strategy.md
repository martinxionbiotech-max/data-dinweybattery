# Pillar-Cluster Content Strategy

This is the internal content architecture for the DINWEY Battery Knowledge Hub. It maps every
documentation page to one of **five topical pillars**, each with its supporting cluster pages.
The goal is a semantic, internally-linked hierarchy that signals topical authority to both
search engines and AI retrieval systems.

## The Five Pillars

| # | Pillar (authority hub) | Focus | Cluster pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Battery Fundamentals** | Core concepts every buyer must understand | CCA, Reserve Capacity, Ah, EFB, AGM, terminals, 12V/24V |
| 2 | **Standards & Sizing** | JIS / DIN / BCI — the three regional systems | Group sizes, model numbers, GB codes, cross-reference |
| 3 | **Selection & Buying** | How to choose and buy the right battery | Buying guide, brand comparison, warranty, datasheet, OEM |
| 4 | **Maintenance & Life** | Keep batteries alive and diagnose failure | Testing, charging, jump-start, winter failure, longevity |
| 5 | **Applications & Fleets** | Match battery to duty cycle | Long-haul, cold-chain, mining, semi-trailer, generator |

## Internal-Link Architecture

The pillar pages act as **hubs**: each one links down to every cluster page in its pillar, and
each cluster page links back up to its pillar plus laterally to 2–3 related clusters. This
creates a dense, semantically meaningful link graph that:

1. **Signals topical authority** — a page linking all CCA-related clusters is recognized as an authority hub
2. **Passes PageRank** — internal links concentrate ranking power on the 5 pillars
3. **Improves AI retrieval** — AI systems follow the same link graph to understand topic scope
