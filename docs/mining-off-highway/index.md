# Batteries for Mining & Off-Highway Equipment

**TL;DR** — Mining and off-highway equipment subjects batteries to extreme vibration, dust,
temperature swings and deep cycling — enough to halve a battery's life (4 years on-road vs 2
years off-highway). Choose rugged heavy-duty batteries; AGM is often the best fit for its
sealed, vibration-tolerant design.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Off-highway duty can halve battery life** — a battery that lasts 4 years on a highway truck can fail in 2 on a mining haul truck, because the mechanical and thermal stress is relentless, not because the chemistry differs.
2. **AGM's sealed design matters as much as its vibration resistance** — a vented flooded battery loses electrolyte on steep grades and is vulnerable to conductive dust; AGM's sealed design removes both risks, and its 600–1200 cycle life (vs 200–300 for flooded) matches the deep cycling.
3. **Large mining haulers still run 24V** — two 12V batteries in series with 850–1100+ CCA for the big diesel, and the matched-pair rule (same model, age, rating) still applies when you replace them.
4. **Inspect hold-downs at every service and load-test at least 2× a year** — vibration loosens brackets, and harsh conditions halve life (4 years → 2), so the annual-test habit is not enough here.
5. **Flooded Pb-Sb is "good", AGM is "best", EFB is only mid-tier** — AGM's 600–1200 cycle life and sealed design make it the default recommendation for off-highway, not a premium add-on.

## The Harshest Duty Cycle

Off-highway equipment faces conditions that quickly destroy ordinary batteries:

1. **Extreme vibration** — damages plates and causes internal shorts
2. **Dust and debris** — contaminates and corrodes
3. **Temperature extremes** — from freezing nights to hot engine bays
4. **Deep cycling** — frequent starts and heavy loads

The combination is what makes off-highway service so demanding. A battery that would last four
years on a highway truck can fail in two on a mining haul truck — not because the chemistry is
different, but because the mechanical and thermal stress is relentless.

## Technology Choice

| Technology | Suitability for off-highway |
|---|---|
| Flooded (heavy-duty) | Good with robust Pb-Sb thick-plate construction |
| AGM | Best — sealed, vibration-resistant, maintenance-free |
| EFB | Mid-tier, for lighter off-highway duty |

AGM is often the preferred choice for mining and off-highway because its absorbent glass mat
construction resists vibration and it is fully sealed against dust and spillage.

### Why the Sealed Design Matters

Mining and quarry equipment operates at extreme angles and in clouds of conductive dust. A
vented flooded battery can lose electrolyte on steep grades and is vulnerable to dust
contamination around the caps. AGM's sealed, pressure-regulated design removes both risks,
which is why it is the default recommendation for the harshest off-highway environments.

## Selection Criteria

- **Group size** — match the equipment's battery tray (JIS N150/N200 or equivalent)
- **CCA** — match the engine's cranking requirement (large diesels need 850–1100+ CCA)
- **Vibration resistance** — prioritize rugged construction
- **Sealed design** — protect against dust and tilt

## Maintenance in Harsh Environments

- Inspect hold-down brackets frequently (vibration loosens them)
- Keep terminals clean — dust accelerates corrosion
- Load-test regularly — harsh conditions shorten life
- Check charging — off-highway alternators work hard

## A Note on 24V Mining Systems

Large mining trucks and off-highway haulers commonly run 24V starting systems (two 12V
batteries in series), for the same reason road-going semis do: halving the current for the same
power. The same matched-pair rule applies — always replace both batteries together, same
model, age and rating. See [24V wiring guide](../24v-wiring-guide/index.md) and
[12V vs 24V systems](../12v-vs-24v/index.md).
