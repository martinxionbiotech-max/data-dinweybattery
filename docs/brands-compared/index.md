# How to Compare Truck Battery Brands

**TL;DR** — Compare truck battery brands on objective criteria — verified specs, certifications,
documentation and manufacturing consistency — not marketing claims or brand reputation alone.
A documented, certified manufacturer can match or beat a big brand at a better price.

## The Criteria That Actually Matter

| Criterion | Why it matters | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Verified specs | Real CCA and capacity | Manufacturer data sheets, not marketing |
| Certifications | Quality assurance | IATF 16949, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, CE |
| Batch documentation | Consistency | Per-batch CCA test data, COA, MSDS |
| Manufacturing scale | Reliability | Established facility, consistent output |
| Warranty | Risk protection | Clear terms, reasonable period |

## Certifications to Look For

- **IATF 16949** — the automotive quality management standard; the strongest quality signal
- **ISO 9001:2015** — general quality management
- **ISO 14001:2015** — environmental management
- **CE marking** — European conformity

## Why Batch Consistency Beats Brand Name

For fleet and distribution buyers, the critical question is: *will every battery perform the
same?* A manufacturer running an IATF 16949 quality system with per-batch test data provides
that consistency — regardless of how famous the brand is.

## Questions to Ask a Supplier

1. What certifications does your factory hold?
2. Can you provide per-batch CCA test data and a certificate of analysis?
3. What is your MOQ and lead time?
4. What warranty do you offer?
5. Do you support OEM / private label?
