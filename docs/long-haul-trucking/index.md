# Batteries for Long-Haul Trucking Fleets

**TL;DR** — Long-haul trucks run on 24V systems with high CCA and need batteries that
withstand constant vibration. Specify the right group size and CCA for your market, test
proactively, and replace on a schedule to keep trucks moving.

## The Demands of Over-the-Road Duty

Long-haul trucks place three specific demands on batteries:

1. **High cranking current** — large diesel engines need 850–1100+ CCA
2. **Constant vibration** — road vibration damages plates over time
3. **Extended runtime** — auxiliary loads (sleeper cab, telematics, refrigeration) may run
   while the engine is off

## System Configuration

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| System voltage | 24V (two 12V batteries in series) |
| JIS market | 190H52 (N200) or 145G51 (N150) |
| DIN market | 60038 (DIN100) or 58827 (DIN88) |
| CCA need | 850–1100+ CCA |
| Technology | Flooded for standard duty; AGM for high vibration |

## Vibration and Battery Life

Vibration is a leading cause of premature truck battery failure. Heavy-duty batteries use
robust plate construction (often Pb-Sb thick-plate for the JIS heavy-duty range) to resist it.
For high-vibration or demanding applications, AGM offers superior vibration resistance.

## Fleet Best Practices

1. **Standardize** on one or two group sizes and CCA ratings
2. **Test proactively** — load-test at least annually, before winter
3. **Track age** — replace at 3–5 years or when load-test fails
4. **Match technology to duty** — flooded for standard, AGM for demanding
