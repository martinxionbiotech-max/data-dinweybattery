# About This Knowledge Hub

The **DINWEY Battery Knowledge Hub** is the official technical documentation
sub-site of [dinweysbattery.com](https://dinweysbattery.com).

a battery OEM factory founded in 2002.

## What This Hub Is For

This hub provides technical documentation to help engineers, fleet managers,
distributors and buyers specify the right truck battery:

- Battery standards (JIS, DIN/EN, BCI, SAE J537)
- Cold Cranking Amps (CCA) and Reserve Capacity (RC) explained
- Selection guides and comparison tables
- Maintenance and troubleshooting
- Application scenarios (long-haul, cold-chain, mining, semi-trailer, generator)

## Our Editorial Standards

1. **Sourced specifications** — all CCA, capacity and dimension values come from
   Chengguang Energy technical documentation under an IATF 16949 quality system.
2. **No unverifiable claims** — where a value depends on the vehicle, we say so and
   direct you to your manual.
3. **Team authorship** — content is written and reviewed by the DINWEY Technical Team.
   We do not publish attributed articles under invented individual experts.
4. **Transparent dates** — every article carries publication and update dates.
