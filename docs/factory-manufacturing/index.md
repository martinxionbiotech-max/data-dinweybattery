# How DINWEYS Batteries Are Made & Tested

**TL;DR** — DINWEYS is produced end-to-end by Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd., a manufacturer
founded in 2002 that controls the full chain from plate manufacturing to testing and packaging.
Every batch passes five on-site laboratories and a five-stage quality-control chain under an
IATF 16949 system.

## Key Takeaways

1. **In-house, end-to-end** — plate manufacturing, assembly, testing and packaging all run on the same 18 automated lines; nothing is outsourced and re-badged.
2. **Five laboratories, not one** — chemical, physical, electrical, life-cycle and environmental labs each verify a different dimension of the battery.
3. **Five-stage QC, not a final spot-check** — quality is controlled at incoming inspection, in-process, finished-product, pre-shipment and traceability stages.
4. **Per-batch documentation** — COA, MSDS and inspection reports ship with every order, so a buyer can verify a batch rather than trust a claim.
5. **Traceability is the honest part** — batch-level tracking from raw material to container is what makes recalls (if ever needed) possible and import compliance straightforward.

## The Factory

DINWEYS is the truck and heavy-duty starting-battery brand of **Chengguang Power Tech Co., Ltd.**,
a battery manufacturer founded in 2002 and based in Jinzhou City, Shijiazhuang, Hebei Province,
China.

| Fact | Detail |

| Founded | 2002 |
| Facility | 200,000 m² |
| Production | 18 automated assembly lines |
| Export | 70+ countries |
| Focus | Truck / heavy-duty / commercial-vehicle starting batteries |

## The Manufacturing Chain

Chengguang Power Tech controls the entire production chain in-house. A DINWEYS battery is not
assembled from outsourced parts — it is produced end-to-end:

1. **Grid casting** — lead alloy grids form each plate's structure.
2. **Paste mixing** — lead oxide paste is mixed to a controlled recipe.
3. **Pasting** — paste is applied to the grids to form the plates.
4. **Curing** — plates are dried and hardened under controlled conditions.
5. **Formation** — the initial charge converts the plates into active material.
6. **Assembly** — plates are welded into elements and stacked into cells.
7. **Testing** — CCA, capacity, leak and dimensional checks before packing.

## The Five On-Site Laboratories

Every batch is verified across five laboratories before release:

| Laboratory | What it verifies |
|---|---|
| Chemical Lab | Raw material analysis, paste composition, electrolyte purity |
| Physical Testing Lab | Plate strength, separator porosity, container integrity |
| Electrical Testing Lab | CCA, reserve capacity, charge acceptance, high-rate discharge |
| Life Cycle Testing | JIS/DIN/BCI cycle life, corrosion resistance, overcharge endurance |
| Environmental Chamber | Temperature cycling (−40°C to +80°C), humidity, vibration |

## The Five-Stage Quality-Control Chain

Quality is not a final spot-check — it runs the length of the chain:

1. **Incoming material inspection** — lead ingots, sulfuric acid, polypropylene and separator materials are tested before production.
2. **In-process QC** — all 18 lines are monitored in real time for paste density, plate weight, welding integrity and electrolyte fill levels.
3. **Finished product testing** — every batch undergoes CCA, reserve capacity, charge acceptance, leak test and visual inspection.
4. **Pre-shipment audit** — random sampling per AQL standards; packaging, labelling and documentation verified.
5. **Traceability** — batch-level tracking from raw material to container, with full documentation for import compliance.

## Documentation That Ships With Every Order

| Document | What it proves |
|---|---|
| COA (Certificate of Analysis) | Per-batch measured CCA, capacity, RC |
| MSDS (Safety Data Sheet) | Chemical and handling safety |

Third-party lab testing (SGS, TÜV, BV) is available at the buyer's request.
