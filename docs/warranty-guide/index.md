# Truck Battery Warranty Guide

**TL;DR** — Battery warranties cover manufacturing defects and premature failure, typically as
a free-replacement period followed by a pro-rated period. Coverage varies (commonly 12–36
months for heavy-duty batteries). Read the terms and keep your records.

## What Warranties Cover

A battery warranty typically covers:

- **Manufacturing defects** — internal shorts, premature failure
- **Premature capacity/CCA loss** — if the battery fails a load test within the period

It typically does **not** cover:

- Damage from misuse or deep discharge
- Physical damage or incorrect installation
- Use outside the battery's specification

## Free Replacement vs Pro-Rated

| Warranty type | How it works |
|---|---|
| Free replacement | Full replacement at no cost during the initial period |
| Pro-rated | Partial credit toward a replacement, based on elapsed time |

Many warranties combine both: a free-replacement period, then a pro-rated period.

## Typical Coverage Periods

| Battery type | Common warranty range |
|---|---|
| SLI (flooded) | 12–24 months |
| EFB | 18–36 months |
| AGM | 24–48 months (often longer) |

*Coverage varies by manufacturer — always confirm the specific terms.*

## How to Protect Your Claim

1. **Keep proof of purchase** and installation date
2. **Record the vehicle and application**
3. **Have a load test done** — claims usually require confirming the battery failed
4. **Read the exclusions** — know what voids coverage
