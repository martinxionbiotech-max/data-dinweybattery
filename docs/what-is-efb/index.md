# What Is an EFB Battery (for Commercial Vehicles)?

**TL;DR** — EFB (Enhanced Flooded Battery) is a step up from standard flooded lead-acid: it
adds carbon-enhanced plates and a polyester scrim separator for better charge acceptance and
cycle life. It sits between flooded and AGM in both performance and price.

## EFB vs Standard Flooded vs AGM

| Specification | SLI (Flooded) | EFB | AGM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology | Flooded lead-acid | Enhanced flooded | Absorbent Glass Mat |
| Cycle life @ 25°C | 200–300 | 400–600 | 600–1200 |
| Charge acceptance | 1.0× | 1.5–2.0× SLI | 2.5–3.5× SLI |
| Start-stop compatible | No | Yes (entry-level) | Yes (full) |
| Relative cost | 1.0× | 1.3–1.5× | 1.8–2.2× |

*Data from Chengguang Energy technical documentation.*

## When to Choose EFB

- **Entry-level start-stop systems** — where standard flooded is not durable enough
- **Mid-tier commercial vehicles** — needing better cycle life than flooded without AGM's cost
- **Higher electrical loads** — better charge acceptance for modern vehicles

## When to Choose Something Else

- **Conventional, gentle duty** — standard flooded is sufficient and cheapest
- **Demanding, high-vibration, cold-climate** — AGM's superior performance justifies its cost
